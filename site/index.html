How it works

4 moves. In order. No detours.

  1. 01

    Find the idea worth building

    Pull from what people already ask you for. A problem you've solved more than twice beats a market you have to invent.

  2. 02

    Shape it into 1 product

    One promise, one format, one buyer. This is the pass that stops a good idea becoming a 9-month course nobody finishes.

  3. 03

    Build it with AI

The honest answers.

I don't have an audience yet.

You don't need one to start, and this doesn't pretend otherwise. Your first customers are the people who already know your work: past clients, the person who asked you a question last week, the group chat you answer for free. That's a list. It's just not on a platform yet.

I don't have an idea.

Step 1 is entirely about finding one, and it looks backwards through work you've already done rather than forwards at a blank page. Most people have 3 ideas sitting in their sent folder and can't see them because they're too close.

Isn't this market saturated?

At the general level, yes. Nobody needs another course on making courses. What isn't saturated is the specific thing you know for the specific people you already serve. Step 2 exists to make the product narrow enough that saturation stops being the question.

Is this just AI writing it for me?

No, and a product written that way is obvious to read. AI drafts and formats. You decide what's true, what's worth including, and what you'd stake your name on. Your judgement is the product. The tool only makes it faster to get out of your head.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Then you'll have found out in a weekend instead of a year, which is most of the point. The first step is a 20-minute pass designed to kill bad ideas early, including mine.

Learn it once. Use it on everything you make from here.

The first product is the hard one. After that you're running the same process again with a better idea.
