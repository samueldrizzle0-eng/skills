The system

A repeatable way to ship the product you've been meaning to make.

4 steps, in order, each one ending in something you can point at. It isn't a course about digital products. It's the process for making 1.

Who this is for, and who it isn't.

This is for you if

  • You already deliver something people pay for, and you're doing it by the hour.
  • People ask you the same question often enough that you have a stock answer.
  • You've started a product before and stalled somewhere in the middle.
  • You want 1 finished thing, not a content strategy.

This isn't for you if

  • You haven't done the work yet. There's nothing to package, and no process fixes that.
  • You want passive income without making something first. That isn't what this is.
  • You're looking for a big launch. This is built for a small, quiet first sale.
  • You want someone to write it for you. AI drafts, but you decide.

What actually changes.

Before

  • A folder of half-started documents and 3 ideas you can't choose between.
  • Income that stops the week you do.
  • "I'll do it properly when I have time," repeated for a year.

After

  • 1 product, finished, with a page and a way to pay for it.
  • A process you can run again without asking anyone what to do next.
  • Your first sale, which changes how the second one feels.

What's inside.

The Idea Filter

A 20-minute pass that tells you whether an idea is worth your weekend, before you spend it. It's designed to kill ideas, which is the part that saves you months.

The Prompt Library

The exact prompts used to draft, structure, and edit a product, written for this job rather than the generic ones you end up arguing with.

Build Templates

Docs, Notion, and page templates for the 5 formats that reliably sell, so you're filling something in rather than starting at a blank page.

The Launch Kit

Sales page copy, pricing frames, checkout, and delivery. Set up once, reused on everything you make after this.

Weekly teardowns

Every week, 1 real digital product taken apart: what it sells, how it was built, what it charges, and the one thing worth stealing. These start the day you join the list.

A digital product is a piece of knowledge or experience packaged once and sold repeatedly.
