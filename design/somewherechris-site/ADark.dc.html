The distance between “I have an idea” and “here is the thing you can buy” is full of decisions nobody taught you how to make. What format? What goes in it? What do you charge? Where does it live after someone pays?

AI closes that gap. It does not replace your thinking — your taste and your experience are still the product. It removes the part where you stall for six months because you have never done this before.

How it works

Four moves, in order.

01

Find the idea worth building

Pull from what people already ask you for. You are looking for a problem you have solved more than twice, not a market you have to invent.

02

Shape it into one product

One promise, one format, one buyer. The scoping pass that stops a good idea turning into a nine-month course nobody finishes.

03

Build it with AI

Draft, structure, design and package in days instead of months — using prompts written for the job, not generic ones you have to fight.

04

Put it up for sale

Pricing, sales page, delivery, and a plan for the first ten customers — the part most people skip and then blame the product.
