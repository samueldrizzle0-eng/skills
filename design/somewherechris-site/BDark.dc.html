01 / The problem

The idea is rarely what stops you.

The distance between “I have an idea” and “here is the thing you can buy” is full of decisions nobody taught you how to make. What format. What goes in it. What to charge. Where it lives after someone pays.

AI closes that gap. It does not replace your judgement — your judgement is the product. It removes the six months you would otherwise spend stalling because you have never done this before.
