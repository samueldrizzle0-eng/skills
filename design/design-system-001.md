# SomewhereChris Design System — 001

**Prepared for:** Sam (SomewhereChris digital product brand)
**Purpose:** Visual foundation for website, digital products, and Substack assets

---

## 1. Brand Feel

Trustworthy, premium, calm, welcoming, minimal.

Inspired by Tally.so's document-style, no-fuss layout: lots of white space, plain confident typography, muted rather than bright colors, clarity over decoration. Every visitor should immediately understand what they're looking at and what they're being offered — no visual noise competing with the message.

---

## 2. Color Palette

**Primary**

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Teal | `#008080` | Headers, buttons, links, primary brand color |
| Soft Nyanza Green | `#D6F6D6` | Accents, highlight backgrounds, success states |

**Neutrals** (do the majority of the visual work)

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink Black | `#1A1A1A` | Body text |
| Charcoal Gray | `#4A4A4A` | Secondary text, captions |
| Warm Off-White | `#FAFAF7` | Page background |
| Light Gray Border | `#E5E5E0` | Dividers, card borders |

**Usage rule:** Neutrals handle ~80% of the visual weight. Teal and green are accents used sparingly — for CTAs, key highlights, and moments you want the eye to land on. This keeps things premium instead of loud.

---

## 3. Typography

- **Headings:** Inter (or Söhne as a paid alternative) — Semibold/Bold
- **Body text:** Inter Regular, or system default sans-serif for fast load times
- **Optional accent:** a monospace font (e.g. IBM Plex Mono) for small labels, timestamps, or "how it works" steps — echoes Tally's clean, technical honesty

**Sizing guide**

| Element | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | 40–48px | Tight line height |
| H2 | 28–32px | |
| H3 | 20–22px | |
| Body | 16–18px | Relaxed line height (1.5–1.6) for readability |
| Captions/labels | 13–14px | Charcoal Gray |

---

## 4. Layout Principles

- Generous white space, especially above and below section breaks
- Single-column, document-style flow for long-form pages (mirrors Tally)
- Max content width ~680–760px for text-heavy sections — comfortable reading, like a well-formatted document, not a cluttered site
- Cards/boxes use soft rounded corners (8–12px radius) and a thin Light Gray Border rather than heavy shadows
- One clear call-to-action per section — never compete with multiple CTAs

---

## 5. Components

**Buttons**
- Primary: Deep Teal background, white text, rounded corners, no gradient
- Secondary: transparent background, Teal border and text
- Hover: slightly darker teal, no flashy animation — calm transitions only

**CTA blocks**
- Every content piece and every page section ends with one clear CTA
- Use Soft Nyanza Green as a background highlight behind key CTA text to draw the eye without shouting

**Forms / product delivery pages**
- Step-by-step, document style — one question or one product step visible at a time where possible, echoing Tally's form flow
- Clear "what you get" labeling before any purchase button

---

## 6. Voice-to-Visual Alignment

Voice is calm, story-driven, and straight to the point — visuals should match: no hype colors, no clutter, no over-designed graphics. Let the words and clean layout carry the trust. Premium here means restraint, not luxury flourishes.
